Before you submit

Only submit a claim if the item, date, and general area closely match what you lost. Saving a found item does not reserve it or prove ownership.


Keep clues specific and private

Mention distinct marks or contents inside that only the owner would know. These notes stay private for finder verification—avoid passwords or sensitive document numbers.


What happens next

After you send your claim, you can track its status under My Recovery Claims and coordinate a safe public handover if approved.
